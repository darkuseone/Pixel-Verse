## Шапка YouTube — общая для канала (16:9, 2560×1440; безопасная зона по центру 1546×423)
Канал будет антологией: разные сезоны, города и герои (Флорида, дети на электровелосипедах, нуарный Чикаго, Нью-Йорк и др.), поэтому шапка не привязана к одному мульту.
```
Wide pixel art banner, 16:9, chunky 16-bit style with thick dark-purple outlines. A continuous panorama of "America in pixels" under one huge hot-pink and peach sunset sky with a big setting sun, fluffy pixel clouds and a few white sparkles. From left to right the scene smoothly changes: a pastel Florida suburb with palm trees and a pink plastic lawn flamingo; a neon-lit street with a couple of cartoon teens on glowing e-bikes; a moody noir Chicago corner with a brick building, fire escape, an elevated train and a detective in a trench coat under a street lamp; a New York corner with yellow taxi, hot-dog cart, steaming manhole and a skyline in the distance. Small quirky cartoon characters with dumb-but-lovable faces are scattered in the scene (a sunburnt guy with a mullet and aviator sunglasses, a smug woman with a sun visor, a deadpan alligator peeking out of a pond), none of them dominating. Keep the central horizontal band clean and readable with simple shapes so a channel name can be placed there later; put the busy details on the far left and far right, and keep the top and bottom 25% calm (sky and ground). Palette: hot pink, bubblegum pink, peach, aqua, white and deep purple accents, bright, saturated, playful, with pink as the dominant color. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.
```
Если получится слишком пёстро — короткая версия (то же, но без городов, один «розовый закат над пиксельной Америкой»):
```
Wide pixel art banner, 16:9, chunky 16-bit style with dark-purple outlines. A dreamy hot-pink and peach sunset sky with a big sun and pixel clouds over a stylized American skyline silhouette (palm trees, a water tower, city towers, a diner sign without text), a few tiny quirky cartoon characters in the distance. Calm clean center band for a channel name, details only at the far left and right. Palette: hot pink, bubblegum pink, peach, aqua, white, deep purple. No text, no logos, no watermark.
```
Шапку под конкретный мульт (например, Sunny Palms HOA) можно делать отдельно для плейлиста, не для канала.
